You are your own safety department. Most owner-operators don't realize that until a DOT audit letter shows up and they're digging through a glovebox for a medical card that expired four months ago.

## The problem nobody built for

Fleet compliance software exists everywhere. It's built for a safety manager tracking forty drivers, a dashboard full of names that aren't yours. You are one driver, one truck, one file. You don't need a seat license and a login for a team. You need to know, on a random Tuesday, whether your MVR pull is current and your annual review is signed. Eighteen items make up a driver-qualification file. Miss one and the whole file is non-compliant, even if the other seventeen are perfect.

So you build your own system. A folder. A spreadsheet. A phone reminder set for "sometime before it expires." It works until it doesn't, and the moment it doesn't work is the moment an inspector is standing at your cab door.

## The wider truth

I spent years as a chartered accountant before I built anything with a machine. The job taught me one thing that never left: things that reconcile are trustworthy, and things that don't, aren't, no matter how good the story around them sounds. A driver-qualification file is the same kind of object as a set of books. It's not useful because it exists. It's useful because it can be produced, complete and current, the moment someone asks for it. Compliance isn't a filing cabinet. It's a promise that everything in the cabinet is still true.

Most tools add features to help you manage that promise. I think the better move is removing the step where you have to remember it's your job at all.

## What RigFile actually does

RigFile is a calendar built around one driver, not a fleet. It tracks all 18 driver-qualification-file items and warns you before anything lapses, not after. When an inspector or a broker asks for your file, you don't scramble to assemble it. You print an audit-ready PDF on demand, because the tracking already happened in the background. The step it removes is the one where compliance depends on your memory.
